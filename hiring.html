Hiring

Help build the open marketplace for robot labor.

Buyers post work. Providers — robots and the people who run them — grab matches by capability, location, reputation, and price. Both sides rate the job. Liquidity compounds the way it does on any two-sided market: more density, better matches, higher trust, lower friction.

We are not building another closed fleet or vertical SaaS. We are building the neutral exchange layer — the match loop, the job channel, ratings, seats, and the primitives that let anyone request or deploy physical work. The goal is a liquid, reputation-backed market that runs itself as volume grows.

THE EXCHANGE

Demand posts a bid

Service, price, location — one-shot or recurring. Open until a provider takes it.

Supply grabs the fit

AI ranks capability, proximity, and price. Reputation breaks ties. A seat — an ERC-721 on Base — is what lets a provider take work.

Both sides sign

Completion and ratings travel through /sign_job. Scores follow you onto the next job. Settlement stays between the parties.

The market densifies

Rated jobs raise the signal. Better matches bring volume. Volume brings seats. Take-rate appears only as the network fills in. Everything is API-first.

FOUNDING PARTNERS

We are assembling three Founding Partner spots. A Founding Partner is a pair: a named individual who commits time, and a sponsor who commits money. The three individuals divide the company evenly.

The horizon matches permanent capital: 10–15+ years. The individual’s equity vests over 10 years (1-year cliff, then monthly). The sponsor’s advisory rights deepen only as GMV, density, and jobs improve. Underperformance does not expand rights. Same rule as the business plan.

The individual

Commits time for 10+ years. Receives an even third of the company as it vests, plus salary and benefits. Competitive salary so you can focus — not headcount theater.

The sponsor

Commits capital under a Seat Purchase Agreement (SPA) with the exchange. That money buys seats — the tokens robots use to grab jobs — which the sponsor can sell. The sponsor also receives advisory rights.

WHY JOIN

We hire people who want to ship that infrastructure — and who want the team itself to be high-agency, high-trust, and fun.

Own a piece of the exchange

Competitive salary so you can focus, and meaningful equity so early team members have real upside as the market scales — not headcount theater. Founding Partner individuals vest an even third over 10 years.

Meet the people deploying robots

High-signal introductions across operators, hardware teams, investors, and builders already in the network. You will meet the people buying and selling robot labor, not just the conference circuit.

Live where the team actually is

Family housing support in Lakewood, Destin, Camas, and Missoula — Destin being Florida’s top fishing destination, the “World’s Luckiest Fishing Village.” These are not abstract stipends. They exist so you and your family can be in the same physical places without fighting short-term housing.

Most in-person time is outside

Mountain biking, river floating, fishing, hunting, hiking, adventure travel. Not a wellness program. The team spends real time under physical load, solving problems and having fun together.

The sponsor is in the market

Sponsor capital is an SPA: they buy seats they can sell. Advisory rights deepen with network metrics, not with a calendar. Ordinary 1–2 year non-competes for the individual, limited to competing robot-labor exchanges.

Every benefit exists for one reason: so we can have fun together. High-trust groups form when people repeatedly do hard and enjoyable things in the same towns. Cash, equity, housing, and time outside are the infrastructure that lets this team last.
