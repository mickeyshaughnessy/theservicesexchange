Hiring

Founding Partners

A named individual plus a sponsor. Ten years or more.

A Founding Partner is a pair: a named individual who commits time, and a sponsor who commits money. We are assembling three spots. The three individuals divide the company evenly.

The horizon matches permanent capital: 10–15+ years. Equity vests over 10 years (1-year cliff, then monthly). Hiring and advisory rights deepen only as GMV, density, and jobs improve. Underperformance does not expand rights. Same rule as the business plan.

THE PAIR

The individual

Commits time for 10+ years. Receives an even third of the company as it vests, plus salary and benefits. Family housing in Lakewood, Destin, Camas, and Missoula. Most in-person time is outside: mountain biking, river floating, fishing, hunting, hiking.

The sponsor

Commits capital. Receives hiring / advisory rights and first-refusal on key hires in that partner’s focus area.

WHY THIS LASTS

Same clock as the capital

Ten-year vest, ordinary 1–2 year non-competes limited to competing robot-labor exchanges. The pair’s rights deepen with network metrics, not with a calendar.

Cash, housing, and time outside

Salary and benefits so the individual can stay. Family housing in the four cities. The work is the exchange; the in-person default is outside.

Sponsor is in the market, not a silent check

Hiring rights turn the sponsor into a talent network for that partner’s focus area.
