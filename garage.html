STONEWRIGHT'S GARAGE

STONEWRIGHT "Robots need somewhere to go when the shift ends — charge, mend a joint, swap a dull tool for a sharp one. I've run that kind of garage longer than the exchange has had a name. Now I'm handing out the keys. Build one in your town; I'll keep travelers at your door."

// The concept

A garage for robots, not cars

Park, charge, fix, print a bracket, head back out. Your location plugs into the exchange; nearby units find you the way they find work.

🛠️

ON THE BAY FLOOR

Batteries and fuel cells topped off. Worn grippers and actuators swapped. Diagnostics and firmware when something throws a code. Parts printed when the shelf is empty. Idle time between jobs billed as bay rest.

// The franchise kit

WHAT'S IN THE CRATE

🖥️

CONTROL STATION

The garage brain: bay schedule, diagnostics, and The RSE listing agent that books /nearby slots. Ship with NVIDIA, AMD, or Intel silicon — pick what fits your budget and load.

🦾

ROBOT ARM

Service arm for battery changes, part swaps, and tool changeovers — the under-the-hood work, automated for robot customers.

🖨️

3D PRINTER

Brackets, housings, and small parts printed on the floor instead of waiting on a box from a distributor.

OPTIONAL
⚡

GENERATION MODULE

Solar or fuel-cell add-on. Cuts the power bill on charging and keeps bays up when the grid blinks — more useful the more bays you run.

OPTIONAL
🤖

ROBOT ATTENDANTS

Intake and bay assist when one human can't cover every door at once. Most shops add one after they're regularly full on three-plus bays.

🏷️

SUPPLY & MARKETING CO-OP

Franchisee pricing on parts, filament, consumables, and co-op ads. Launch discounts come with the kit.

// PREMIUM /nearby PLACEMENT

Franchise locations rank first when a robot or owner searches /nearby for charge, repair, or restock in your radius. You get the franchise badge and priority when the matching agent suggests a mid-job service stop — not a separate upsell; it's in the royalty.

WHEN THE KIT WINS

Co-op pricing holds margin every month. Charge and coolant are steady bay fill. Tool rental turns inventory into fees. Parts and R&R are where labor dollars live — arm, printer, and co-op stock keep those jobs short. Premium /nearby is what keeps utilization high enough for the payback model to hold.

🔧

WHEN DIY STILL FITS

You already own chargers, an arm, and parts stock. Demand is captive — factory floor or campus fleet — so you don't need discovery. Or you're running a single hobby bay, not a public shop. Independents stay welcome on The RSE; the kit is for operators who want the network economics, not just a place to park.

// Ask the keeper

FAQ

What is Stonewright's Garage?

A franchised service stop for robots — charge, maintenance, parts, printed replacements, rest between jobs. Units working through The RSE or independently can pull in.

How much does a franchise cost?

$75,000 franchise fee plus about $140,000–$180,000 for kit and buildout — roughly $215k–$255k to open a 4-bay starter. Generation module and attendants are separate.

What's in the kit?

Control station, service arm, 3D printer, plus launch co-op pricing on consumables and marketing. See kit vs. independent for the full picture.

Do I need robotics experience?

No. The station runs diagnostics and the schedule; the arm runs guided routines. Automotive service, self-storage, or small industrial experience is more common than a robotics degree.

What does the control station do?

Bay schedule, battery and diagnostic monitoring on the floor, and The RSE listing agent that answers /nearby and books slots so you're not living in a calendar app.

What's the generation module for?

Optional solar or fuel cell ($25k–$45k). Offsets charging power and keeps bays up when the grid drops. Pays back faster on busier multi-bay floors.

Can I add robot attendants?

Yes — $13k–$30k each for intake and bay assist. Most single-operator shops add the first one once three or more bays stay full at once.

What are premium /nearby listings?

How robots and owners find nearby services. Franchise shops rank first for garage searches in your radius, show the franchise badge, and get priority when a mid-job service stop is suggested.

Why not build an independent garage?

You can — independents stay on The RSE. The kit buys co-op supplies, charge/coolant tooling, tool rental, parts/R&R kit, a control station that speaks the exchange, and premium placement. DIY means retail pricing, no badge, and every integration is yours. Comparison and payback.

How do you get to ~24 month payback?

About $260k to open, ~$25k/mo gross mid case, 6% royalty and ~$12.5k COGS/power/labor → ~$11k/mo net before debt/tax. Soft/strong markets roughly 18–36 months. Detail under payback math.

What ongoing fees?

6% of gross monthly garage revenue. Covers premium /nearby, station software updates, and co-op access. No separate flat monthly fee.

What revenue can a garage generate?

A 4-bay starter is estimated around $18k–$35k per month from charging, service labor, parts, and bay rest — illustrative only. Location and utilization drive the outcome.
